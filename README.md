## Project 1 at a glance

**The challenge:** A fitness startup wants an app for office professionals who can't find time to exercise. Before any screen is designed, the team needs to understand the user's pain.

**What I created:**
- Problem identification: why existing fitness apps fail (workouts too long, loud and gamified interfaces, needing a gym)
- An illustrative user persona, Alex Morgan, whose core goal is mental clarity and stress relief rather than weight loss
- A 4-quadrant empathy map: what the user says, thinks, does and feels
- Pain points, core needs and design opportunities

**Key lesson:** good UX starts with understanding people, not pixels.
