# Project 1: The Empathy Map

**UI/UX Design Internship at DecodeLabs, Batch 2026, Week 1**
**By:** Anu Hajam

## The challenge

A fitness startup wants an app for busy office professionals who struggle to find time to exercise. Before designing any screen, the team needs to understand the user's pain.

## What I created

- **Problem identification:** why existing fitness apps fail (workouts too long, loud and gamified interfaces, needing a gym)
- **User persona:** Alex Morgan, 34, Senior Product Manager. Core goal: mental clarity and stress relief, not weight loss
- **Empathy map:** what the user says, thinks, does and feels
- **Pain points and design opportunities:** for example 2-minute micro-routines and calm, guilt-free progress
